About Us

What: Student registration is the official process...

When: Registration takes place at the beginning of each semester or academic year, typically announced on the university's website or notice board.

How: Students fill out a digital registration form with personal information, select courses based on their semester, and submit required academic records. The system validates and stores their data securely.

Why: Registration ensures that students are officially recognized by the university, assigned to the correct semester, and allowed to participate in academic activities and access university services.
